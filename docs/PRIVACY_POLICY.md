# ATLAS Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 30 September 2026**

ATLAS is an AI assistant application. This document is a template for the public privacy policy and must be reviewed and published at a public URL before Google Play production release.

## Information used by ATLAS
ATLAS may process account information needed for authentication, conversations and prompts sent to the AI service, files or images the user chooses to attach, task/reminder information, calendar information when the user explicitly connects a calendar, and information needed to operate enabled integrations.

## Third-party services
Depending on the features enabled by the user, ATLAS may communicate with Firebase Authentication, the configured AI/Dify service, Google Calendar, LINE, Open-Meteo/Jina or other explicitly selected services. Their own privacy policies also apply.

## Local data
Conversation history, tasks, memories and settings may be stored locally on the device. The current Android build disables Android backup for this local app data.

## Permissions
Camera and microphone are requested only for features that need them. Notification permission is requested for reminders/notifications. Media permissions are used for user-selected media features.

## Security
Secrets used by server-side integrations should never be embedded in the public application. Before production release, ATLAS must move AI/image API secrets to a server-side backend and rotate any credentials that have been exposed in client builds.
